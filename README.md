# Easy Gardener 🌱

Easy Gardener was born from the idea of letting you design your own garden without relying on external companies. Many people love gardening, but what they're often missing is a clear vision and a plan for how to bring it to life.

## The Idea

The starting point is simple: create a 2D map of your garden in an intuitive way, combined with real photos taken on-site.

From there, an AI agent will take the garden layout together with the photos and generate renderings of the garden based on the plants you've selected — allowing you to preview the visual effect and see how they'd actually look in the context of your own space.

## Features (planned)

- 🗺️ Simple 2D garden layout/mapping tool
- 📸 Integration with real, on-site photos
- 🤖 AI-powered rendering engine
- 🌿 Plant selection with realistic visual preview
- 🏡 See the final result directly in your garden's context — no guesswork, no external designers needed
